# Pyrora Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Pyrora to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming
regardless of experience level, identity, background, or viewpoint. We expect
contributors, maintainers, and community members to act in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, precise, and open to technical disagreement.
- Focus feedback on code, documentation, and ideas—not people.
- Assume good intent, ask clarifying questions, and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the security-reporting process.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
sexualized language or imagery, publishing private information, or bad-faith
conduct are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns through a GitHub private security advisory when
possible, or to a repository maintainer through a private GitHub contact
channel. Reports will be handled discreetly and may result in a warning,
temporary restriction, or removal from project spaces. Retaliation against a
reporter is not acceptable.
